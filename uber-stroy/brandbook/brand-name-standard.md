# Brand naming standard — Uber Stroy

Use the brand name exactly as **Uber Stroy** everywhere.

Mandatory rules:
- Latin characters only.
- Exactly two words: `Uber` then `Stroy`.
- Both words start with an uppercase letter.
- One ordinary space between the words.
- Do not use `UBER STROY`, `Uber Строй`, `Uberстрой`, `UberStroy`, or other variants in visible copy, page titles, metadata, posters, presentations, and documents.
- Preserve this spelling in headings, navigation, footers, filenames where the brand is written out, and social copy.
