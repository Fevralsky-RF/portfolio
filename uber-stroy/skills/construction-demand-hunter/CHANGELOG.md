# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-18
- Создан Construction Demand Hunter как отдельный операционный навык.
- Определены 10 модулей: Source Hunter, Signal Extractor, Company Resolver, Demand Qualifier, Lead Intelligence, Opportunity Scoring, Buyer Finder, Outreach Strategist, Sales Pipeline Manager, Conversion Intelligence.
- Зафиксирована граница с Construction Market Intelligence, Workforce/Skill Intelligence, Sales/Growth и Operations/Dispatch.
- Добавлены data model, evidence/governance, human approval, prompt library и QA gates.
- Scoring weights обозначены как стартовые эвристики, требующие полевой калибровки.
