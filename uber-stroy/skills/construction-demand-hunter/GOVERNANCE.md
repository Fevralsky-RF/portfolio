# Governance & QA — Construction Demand Hunter

## Evidence ladder
E0 unverified; E1 public signal; E2 primary-source verified; E3 counterparty verified; E4 operationally verified; E5 executed/documented. Evidence is assigned per field. Confidence 0–1 is stored separately.

## Data labels
FACT; COMPANY_CLAIM; MODEL_ESTIMATE; HYPOTHESIS; UNKNOWN. Never promote one category without new evidence.

## Source gates
Every source requires documented access method, terms/permission review, rate limits, permitted reuse and retrieval timestamp. No bypass of controls. Stop on unclear access.

## Qualification gates
1. Company identity and role in chain.
2. Fresh and relevant signal.
3. Current demand confirmed or clearly marked potential.
4. Buyer/payer path.
5. External crew/subcontracting acceptance.
6. Workforce capacity and skill evidence.
7. Legal contracting and HSE/admissions.
8. Positive contribution after direct costs.
9. Payment terms and working-capital exposure.

## Human approval
Human approval is mandatory before contact, offer dispatch, supplier commitment, shift assignment, activation of automation or any external write. AI may prepare and recommend, not autonomously commit.

## Privacy
Public repository must not contain personal worker data, private phone lists, documents, recordings, credentials, customer-specific confidential data or private CRM exports. Store only minimum necessary business data in access-controlled systems.

## Audit
Retain source, timestamp, parser/version, raw evidence, decision reason, reviewer and status history. Network errors are not closure. Corrections append provenance rather than silently erase history.

## Release gate
A module is field-ready only after test cases, source permissions, sample review, false-positive/false-negative analysis and owner sign-off. Scores in v1.0.0 are initial heuristics, not trained or validated predictors.
