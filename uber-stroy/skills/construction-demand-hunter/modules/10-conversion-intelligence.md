# M10 — Conversion Intelligence

## Цель
Понять, какие источники и типы сигналов превращаются в подтверждённый спрос, пилоты и оплаченные смены.

## Метрики
- source → unique signal;
- signal → company match;
- signal → qualified external demand;
- buyer reached → need confirmed;
- need confirmed → offer;
- offer → pilot;
- pilot → first fulfilled shift;
- fulfilled → accepted → paid;
- time-to-first-response / time-to-fill;
- fill, show-up, replacement rates;
- gross contribution after direct variable costs;
- payment delay / working-capital days;
- cost per qualified buyer and per paid shift.

## Анализ
Когортируй по source, signal type, geography, work package, buyer segment and period. Показывай denominators, sample size, confidence intervals where appropriate and missing outcomes. Не сравнивай источники с разным охватом без оговорки.

## Выход
Weekly funnel brief; bottleneck diagnosis; source allocation recommendation; experiments; stop/scale decision.

## Главный KPI
Accepted and paid shifts with positive contribution, not raw leads or scraped records.
