# Scoring, Governance & Release Protocol

## Evidence and status
Use E0–E5 and the project lifecycle statuses in M03. Record status date and source.

## Opportunity scoring
Use M13 weights and publish component scores. Hard gates override weighted score. No high score proceeds if legal/HSE, supply, payer, site access or economics is blocked.

## Review roles
- Analyst: source, extraction, calculation, caveats.
- Independent reviewer: high-impact facts, formulas, dedupe.
- Commercial owner: buyer, need, budget, next step.
- Operations owner: supply, skills, logistics, delivery.
- Legal/HSE owner: applicable gates.
- Founder: strategic trade-offs and resource allocation.

## Stop conditions
Stop publication or commitment if the source is inaccessible, calculation irreproducible, duplicates materially distort totals, legal applicability unclear, availability unverified, or the opportunity relies on a false promise.

## Audit
Keep raw records, extraction date, model version, calculation log and decision log. Never silently overwrite historical values.
