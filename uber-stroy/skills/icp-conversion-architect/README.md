# ICP & Conversion Architect v1.0.0

Reusable specialist skill for customer understanding and conversion design.

## Role
ICP & Conversion Architect transforms observed customer problems, buying situations and customer language into a clear value proposition, creative system, landing page, lead-capture flow and measurable CRO experiments.

## Place in the system
- **ICP & Conversion Architect:** ICP, buying roles, JTBD, VoC, pains/outcomes, offer, messaging, creatives, landing pages, forms and CRO.
- **Growth Agent:** channels, ABM, content production cadence, sales pipeline, CRM qualification, growth economics and scale decisions.
- **Sales / Operations / Legal:** own deal execution, fulfilment capacity, contractual model, safety and legal approvals.

The skill complements and does not replace `UBER_STROY_Growth_Agent_Skill_v2_Zero_to_One.md`.

## Files
- `SKILL.md` — full master skill and operating protocol.
- `PROMPT_LIBRARY.md` — reusable prompts for main working modes.
- `SCORING_AND_GOVERNANCE.md` — ICP, offer, CRO and release scorecards.
- `TEMPLATES.md` — repeatable research and handoff worksheets.
- `CHANGELOG.md` — version history.
- `modules/` — task-specific workbooks.

## Use
1. Read `SKILL.md` first.
2. Load only relevant workbooks from `modules/`.
3. Separate observed evidence from inference and hypotheses.
4. Select one ICP and buying situation per conversion asset.
5. Validate claims, delivery capacity and compliance before publication.
6. Pass channel and pipeline execution to Growth Agent.

## Reference project
UBER STROY is the first reference implementation. Its buyer roles (owner, foreman, site master, HR) and supply-side brigade lead are modelled separately. The brigade lead is not mixed into buyer campaigns.
