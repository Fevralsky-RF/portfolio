# Scoring & Governance — ICP & Conversion Architect

## 1. ICP scorecard (1–5)

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem intensity | 15% | Насколько значимо нерешённое состояние? |
| Trigger / urgency | 15% | Есть ли конкретный повод покупать сейчас? |
| Willingness / ability to pay | 15% | Есть ли бюджет, плательщик и ROI? |
| Product / delivery fit | 15% | Может ли бизнес исполнить обещание? |
| Buyer access | 10% | Реально ли достичь участника решения? |
| Frequency / repeatability | 10% | Повторяется ли сценарий? |
| Sales feasibility | 10% | Реалистичны ли цикл, закупка и договор? |
| Risk / compliance fit | 10% | Управляемы ли правовые, операционные и репутационные риски? |

Weighting is a default heuristic. Change it only with a written rationale. A hard legal or fulfilment blocker cannot be offset by a high total score.

## 2. Evidence governance

Use these labels: OBSERVED, REPEATED_PATTERN, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN, DISCONFIRMED. Every direct quote needs source and context. Evidence level and confidence are separate: confidence does not upgrade a hypothesis to fact.

Do not publish or reuse private customer data beyond the permitted purpose. Store only necessary excerpts and redact personal data.

## 3. Offer review (0–5)

- ICP and trigger are precise.
- Outcome is meaningful and understandable.
- Mechanism explains how value is created.
- Differentiation is credible.
- Proof supports the claim.
- Conditions and limitations are transparent.
- CTA matches the next operational step.

A high score cannot override unsupported claims or inability to deliver.

## 4. CRO audit (0–5)

Dimensions: ICP clarity, message match, offer, pain relevance, mechanism, proof, objection handling, CTA, friction, mobile UX, technical integrity, trust/compliance.

0 broken/missing; 1 critically weak; 2 material barrier; 3 acceptable with improvements; 4 strong; 5 compelling and evidenced. Total is a prioritisation aid, not a conversion forecast.

## 5. Experiment governance

- One material variable per experiment when causal attribution matters.
- Log baseline, period, segment, sample, metric and guardrails.
- No winner declaration from tiny samples.
- Assess qualified lead rate, opportunity quality and downstream outcome.
- Record negative and inconclusive results.
- If traffic is insufficient, use interviews and usability tests.

## 6. Release gates

**HOLD** if any of these apply:
- offer makes claims unsupported by evidence;
- business cannot confirm capacity / fulfilment / legal model;
- images missing, corrupted or without usage permission;
- links, buttons, scripts or mobile layout broken;
- form doesn't reach the destination it claims to reach;
- success/error states are not handled;
- data privacy / consent requirements not approved;
- no team owner for handling inbound leads.

**PASS** only when all required gates are verified. Distinguish static-source review from live-browser testing and real form submission.

## 7. UBER STROY buyer/supplier distinction

Buyer side: owner/general contractor, foreman, site master, HR. Supply side: brigade lead / worker-team provider. Separate landing, language, qualification, CRM routing and economics. Do not count candidate workers as buyer leads or buyer demand as available supply.
