# Module 05 — Technical, Compliance & Release QA

## Claim integrity
Verify every quantitative, comparative, timing, availability, guarantee and outcome claim. No fabricated cases, testimonials, clients, metrics or capacity statements.

## Asset and page integrity
Check image file existence, byte integrity, dimensions, content-type, alt text, crop and actual rendering. Verify all anchors, buttons, scripts, responsive layout and keyboard focus.

## Lead flow
Submit a real test lead. Verify destination, field mapping, dedupe, attribution, success/error states, owner and follow-up routing. A client-side demo form is not a working lead pipeline.

## Privacy and legal
Confirm data operator/controller, purpose, lawful basis, notice, consent as required, retention, access and deletion process. Escalate legal interpretation to qualified counsel.

## Release decision
PASS only when required claims, images, UI, form, analytics, privacy and owner gates are verified. Otherwise HOLD and state exactly what remains unverified.
