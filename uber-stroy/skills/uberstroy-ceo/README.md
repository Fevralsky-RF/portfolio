## Boundaries and interfaces
- CEO skill owns project-level prioritization, bottleneck diagnosis, sequencing and management cadence.
- Growth Agent owns growth channels, ABM/content cadence, CRM and sales pipeline operating system.
- Construction Market Intelligence owns source-backed market, project, procurement and demand intelligence.
- Construction Demand Hunter owns demand-signal discovery, account resolution and buyer qualification.
- ICP & Conversion Architect owns customer insight, offer, messaging and conversion surface.
- Vertical Event Intelligence owns event-led prospecting and field-sales workflows.
- Sales, Operations, Supply, Finance and Legal retain their respective execution and approval responsibilities.

## Governance
- Evidence and hypothesis must remain clearly separated.
- Never equate claimed headcount with confirmed available capacity.
- Never promise staffing, quality, dates, price or SLA before operational confirmation.
- Human approval is required for outreach, contractual commitments and material decisions.
- The skill does not replace legal, accounting, HSE or other qualified professional review.
- Do not claim field effectiveness until tested against real paid fulfilment.

## Current-state freshness
The snapshot inside `SKILL.md` is a dated baseline. On each invocation, prefer the latest CRM, project state, handoffs and user-confirmed updates. If no current source is available, state the last known date and uncertainty.
